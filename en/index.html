Apps, tools, automation

Not every project is a website. GingR is an app, and the same workshop turns out small programs that take over recurring handwork: data from one list into the next, a form that spits out a finished file, a script that does the same thing every night so nobody has to do it in the morning. I study computer science, and this is the part of the job I am studying for.

The price follows the work, and we settle it in the first call. There are no packages here, because two tools are rarely the same amount of work. Whatever the scope costs, you hear it before I start.
