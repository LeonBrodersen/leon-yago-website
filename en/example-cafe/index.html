Good to know

Paying
Cash always, girocard debit cards from €10. Not below that, the fee would cost more than the coffee. No credit cards.
Dogs
Welcome inside on a lead. The water bowl is to the left of the door.
Sitting outside
Six tables in the courtyard by the stream, April to October. No roof, so we clear them when it rains.
With children
Two high chairs and a box of building blocks. The changing table is in the ground-floor toilet.
Wi-Fi
Free, the password is on the board behind the counter.
Working on a laptop
Happily in the morning, three tables have sockets. From 2 pm we need the tables for cake guests and ask you to move up.
Allergies
The folder with every ingredient is at the counter, just ask for it. We bake two metres behind the counter, so there is flour in the air.
What we don’t have
No lunch menu, no hot food apart from scrambled eggs. There is an eight-centimetre step at the door. Call out and we put the ramp down. The Mill Room upstairs has no toilet, that means going down a narrow staircase. And on Sundays the cake often runs out around 3 pm.

The Mill Room for parties

Seats
24 people at four long tables for coffee and cake. With a buffet along the wall, 18 seats remain.
Room hire
€45.00 for three hours, €12.00 for each extra hour. Spend €250.00 or more on food and drink and there is no hire fee.
Coffee and cake
Pots of four cups for €5.20, cake at menu prices. We bill afterwards, for what was on the table.
Your own cake
You are welcome to bring one, we provide plates and a cake server. That costs €1.50 per person. Decorations yes, confetti no.
How far ahead
Two weeks is usually enough. For Saturdays in Advent and in May, ask two months ahead.
Enquiries
By phone on 01234 890 122 or by email to hallo@cafe-muehlenstein.example. We reply within two working days. The room is booked once you have our written confirmation.
Music and closing time
There is a socket and room for a small speaker, but no sound system. We finish at 10 pm, people live upstairs.

Getting here

Address
Am Mühlenstein 2, 12345 Beispielstadt
On foot from the market square
Cross the bridge over the stream, then turn right along Mühlenweg by the water. Four minutes, and you can see the mill wheel from the bridge.
By bus
Line 7 to Mühlenweg, then 200 metres west.
By car
There is no parking at the building. You can park for free at the fairground, 200 metres away.
By bike
Six bike stands by the courtyard wall, under the canopy.
