The workout log that compares your weeks.

What GingR does

Was this week better than last week?

GingR answers that from the sets you log. A heatmap colours 17 muscles by how much work they got. When a lift has stalled for weeks, its page says so.

GingR is built and run by ly‑webstudio UG (haftungsbeschränkt) in Berlin, Germany. Managing director: Leon Brodersen.
